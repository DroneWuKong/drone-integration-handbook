Blackbox Log Analyzer

Drop a flight log — Betaflight/iNav .bbl/.csv, ArduPilot .bin, or QuickSilver .json — to see step response, PID error vs throttle, and gyro noise. It runs entirely in your browser; the log never leaves your device.

Analysis engine: AI Wingman — a Kotlin/JS port of PIDtoolbox / PIDscope, licensed GPL-3.0. Corresponding source for wingman-analysis.js is available on request. Handbook content is CC BY-SA 4.0.
