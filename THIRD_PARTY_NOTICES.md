## Editorial sources

Each handbook article should identify primary sources, manufacturer claims, public records, field observations, and adapted material close to the relevant claim. This register does not replace article-level attribution.

## Contributor requirement

A pull request that adds third-party text, code, images, diagrams, screenshots, tables, or datasets must add or update an entry identifying:

- the file and material;
- the author or rights holder;
- the source URL or document;
- the applicable license or written permission;
- whether the material was modified;
- any required attribution or notice.
