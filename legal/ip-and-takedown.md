**Effective date:** July 31, 2026

## License scope

The handbook uses separate licensing for original editorial content and software:

- original Markdown prose, original diagrams, and original reference data are generally offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 unless a file states otherwise;
- original Python, JavaScript, CSS, and HTML/template code is offered under the code license identified in `LICENSE-CODE`;
- third-party text, images, diagrams, datasets, code, trademarks, and specifications remain subject to their own rights and terms.

A repository license cannot grant rights that the publisher or contributor does not own.

## Attribution and provenance

Contributors must identify copied, adapted, quoted, generated, or third-party material and provide the source and applicable license or permission. Manufacturer specifications and public records should be cited precisely enough to locate the source and verification date.

Trademarks and product names are used for identification, comparison, reporting, and commentary. Their use does not transfer trademark rights or imply sponsorship.

## Rights and takedown requests

Send copyright, trademark, privacy, confidential-information, or other rights concerns to [jeremiah@midwestniceuas.com](mailto:jeremiah@midwestniceuas.com). Include:

- your name and contact information;
- the affected page, repository path, or stable link;
- the work, mark, information, or right at issue;
- the basis for your ownership or authority;
- the specific material challenged;
- the action requested;
- supporting documents or source links;
- a statement that the submission is accurate and made in good faith.

Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue. The publisher may request additional information to verify identity, authority, ownership, or the scope of the claim.

## Review and preservation

The publisher may temporarily remove or replace disputed material while investigating. Relevant versions, correspondence, source records, and publication decisions may be preserved to maintain an accurate legal and editorial record. Following review, material may be restored, attributed, revised, licensed, or permanently removed.

## Counter-information and mistakes

A takedown request is not automatically accepted merely because it is submitted. Commentary, criticism, quotation, facts, public records, licenses, permissions, and other legal doctrines may permit use. Conversely, a fair-use label or attribution does not automatically make copying lawful.

This process is a voluntary contact procedure and does not represent that the site qualifies for any particular statutory safe harbor or that the email address is a registered statutory agent.
