# Editorial, Corrections, and Right-of-Reply Policy

**Effective date:** July 31, 2026

The handbook covers technical systems, named companies, public officials, regulations, procurement programs, safety-critical configuration, and defense-adjacent subjects. Accuracy, source preservation, and prompt correction are therefore part of the publication process.

## Evidence standard

Material claims should identify their evidentiary status:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Official-source verified | Supported by a current primary government, standards, or accepted compliance source |
| Manufacturer-reported | Published or supplied by the manufacturer; not independently verified |
| Field-observed | Recorded from a defined configuration, test, or deployment |
| Inference / opinion | Editorial interpretation based on disclosed facts |
| Unknown / program-specific | Evidence is incomplete or the result depends on configuration, contract, agency, or jurisdiction |

Statements such as “compliant,” “safe,” “cleared,” “certified,” “legal,” “EAR99,” or “FOCI clean” should not appear without identifying the authority, item or configuration, source, verification date, and limits of the conclusion.

## Corrections

Send correction requests to [jeremiah@midwestniceuas.com](mailto:jeremiah@midwestniceuas.com) with:

- the article title and repository path or stable link;
- the exact statement being challenged;
- why it is inaccurate, misleading, stale, or incomplete;
- primary documents or other evidence;
- the correction requested;
- any relationship you have to the subject.

A public GitHub issue may also be used for non-sensitive corrections. Do not post confidential, controlled, personal, security-sensitive, or export-restricted material in a public issue.

## Review process

The publisher will ordinarily:

1. preserve the challenged version and source record;
2. assess the cited evidence and seek additional primary sources when needed;
3. contact the affected party when a materially adverse claim requires clarification or right of reply;
4. correct, qualify, retract, or retain the statement based on the record;
5. date material corrections and explain the change when appropriate.

Urgent safety, identity, privacy, legal, or clearly false factual issues receive priority. A request is not guaranteed to produce the outcome requested, but it will be evaluated in good faith.

## Retractions and publication holds

Material may be temporarily replaced with a publication-hold notice when:

- legal or export-control review is required;
- a safety-critical instruction cannot be substantiated;
- a regulatory article is materially stale or inaccurate;
- source ownership or confidentiality is disputed;
- a conflict disclosure is incomplete;
- the evidence record is insufficient for a named adverse claim.

A hold preserves the path and publication history without presenting the prior material as current guidance.

## Right of reply

Companies and identifiable people may provide a concise response and supporting evidence. Relevant, verifiable corrections will be incorporated; disputed editorial conclusions may be updated to present the material facts and the subject's response fairly.

## No pay-to-edit rule

Payment, sponsorship, consulting, donations, equipment access, or commercial pressure do not purchase favorable coverage or prevent correction. Material relationships must be disclosed under the [Affiliations policy](#ch49).
