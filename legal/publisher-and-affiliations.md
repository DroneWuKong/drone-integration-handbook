## Editorial separation

The handbook aims to distinguish:

1. **Official-source verified** — supported by a current regulator, standards body, official list, accepted declaration, or primary manufacturer document.
2. **Manufacturer-reported** — supplied or published by the company and not independently verified.
3. **Field-observed** — documented bench or field observation with the relevant configuration and date.
4. **Inference or opinion** — an interpretation based on disclosed facts.
5. **Unknown or configuration-specific** — insufficient evidence for a reliable conclusion.

A commercial relationship does not convert a manufacturer statement into an independently verified fact.
