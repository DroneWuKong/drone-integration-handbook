## Safe interim guidance

For lawful civilian and commercial operations:

- use only equipment, frequencies, power levels, and configurations authorized for the jurisdiction and operation;
- do not jam, spoof, interfere with, or attempt to locate or attack another transmitter;
- when a control, navigation, or video link becomes unreliable, prioritize a preplanned abort, safe recovery, landing, or other manufacturer-approved contingency;
- document suspected harmful interference and report it through the applicable regulator, spectrum manager, site authority, or public-safety channel;
- use official manufacturer documentation and qualified spectrum or aviation personnel for configuration decisions.

## Review required before republication

Republication requires documented review of:

1. export-control and military end-use implications;
2. FCC and other applicable spectrum restrictions;
3. aviation and physical-safety consequences;
4. source provenance and technical substantiation;
5. separation of lawful interference recognition from offensive or evasive tactics.
