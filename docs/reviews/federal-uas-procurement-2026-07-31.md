## Review objective

Replace the former binary “NDAA compliance” article with a dated screening workflow that separates official ASDA/FAR prohibitions, FASC/SAM party screening, solicitation and agency requirements, DCMA Blue List evidence, configuration evidence, and authorized procurement decisions.

## Material corrections from the withdrawn version

- Removed universal product and vendor “NDAA compliant” conclusions.
- Removed country and founder-birthplace shortcuts.
- Removed unsupported FOCI and export-classification conclusions.
- Removed static covered-entity and product lists that could become stale.
- Separated ASDA/FAR screening from Blue List, solicitation, agency, grant, sanctions, export-control, and cybersecurity questions.
- Added official list-search evidence requirements.
- Added exact-configuration and data-path review.
- Added exemption, exception, and waiver documentation requirements.
- Added an authorized-decision record rather than presenting the handbook as the decision-maker.

## Deliberately excluded scope

- No product, company, country, or configuration is certified.
- No solicitation-specific legal interpretation is provided.
- No determination is made for grants, cooperative agreements, state/local funds, or subcontracts without reviewing their actual terms.
- No Blue List status is generalized beyond the exact official record checked.
- No FOCI, export classification, sanctions, domestic-preference, or cybersecurity determination is made.
- No representation is made on behalf of a contracting officer, program office, agency counsel, or DCMA.

## Qualified-review checklist
