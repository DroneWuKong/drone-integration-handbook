## Use current FAA sources

- [FAA Remote Identification of Drones](https://www.faa.gov/uas/getting_started/remote_id)
- [FAA Drone Registration](https://www.faa.gov/uas/getting_started/register_drone)
- [FAA Remote ID Declaration of Compliance system](https://uasdoc.faa.gov/listDocs)
- [14 CFR Part 89](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-89)

The FAA states that drones required to be registered, or actually registered, generally must comply with Remote ID. A broadcast-module operation has specific limitations, and the applicable Remote ID serial number must be handled through FAA registration procedures. Part 107 and limited-recreational registration are not interchangeable.

No firmware command, wiring diagram, module recommendation, or compliance claim will be republished here until it is verified against current official firmware documentation, an FAA-accepted Declaration of Compliance, and the actual hardware configuration.
