# U.S. Government UAS Procurement Compliance — Accuracy Hold

> **Status:** The prior NDAA/ASDA/Blue UAS article is temporarily withheld as of July 31, 2026 pending legal and source-by-source reconstruction.

<div class="publication-hold">
<strong>Do not use an earlier version to certify a company, product, component, bill of materials, grant purchase, or contract as compliant.</strong> Procurement eligibility is fact-, configuration-, agency-, program-, funding-, contract-, and date-specific.
</div>

## Current official starting points

- [FAR Subpart 40.2 — Security Prohibitions and Exclusions](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/subpart-40.2)
- [SAM.gov American Security Drone Act covered-entity information](https://sam.gov/announcements/american-security-drone-act-covered-foreign-entity-list)
- [DCMA Blue List](https://www.dcma.mil/Blue-List/)
- [Federal Acquisition Regulation](https://www.acquisition.gov/browse/index/far)

A future version will keep distinct:

- statutory or regulatory prohibitions;
- official covered-entity or cleared-product lists;
- agency, contract, grant, waiver, exception, and program requirements;
- manufacturer claims;
- component- and configuration-specific evidence;
- foreign ownership, control, or influence determinations;
- export classification and licensing.

Country of origin, founder birthplace, assembly location, Blue List status, and a vendor's marketing statement are not interchangeable legal tests. No article in this handbook is a procurement certification or legal opinion.
