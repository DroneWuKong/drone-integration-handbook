data, and standards. Bookmark these — they are the primary sources the rest
of this handbook cites for "the rules."*

## Notes for operators

- **Part 107 is the floor, not the ceiling.** State and local law (see NCSL)
  adds restrictions the FAA does not — privacy, trespass, takeoff/landing-site
  rules. Check both before a job.
- **ASIAS is a real corpus.** Incident reports are searchable and worth reading
  before flying an unfamiliar profile — the failure modes repeat.
- **Subscription databases (Janes, Scopus, ScienceDirect) are not listed here**
  — they require institutional access and cannot be cited as open sources.
  See `data/erau_databases.json` in the forge-data repo if you have access.
